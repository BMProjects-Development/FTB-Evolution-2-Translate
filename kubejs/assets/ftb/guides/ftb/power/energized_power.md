  ## <Color id="gold">Solar Panels</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

The higher tiers make far less power than default. Tiers I and II are unchanged.

| Panel | Peak output |
|---|:---:|
| <ItemImage id="energizedpower:solar_panel_3"/> Tier III | 64 FE/t (128) |
| <ItemImage id="energizedpower:solar_panel_4"/> Tier IV | 256 FE/t (2,048) |
| <ItemImage id="energizedpower:solar_panel_5"/> Tier V | 512 FE/t (16,384) |
| <ItemImage id="energizedpower:solar_panel_6"/> Tier VI | 4,096 FE/t (131,072) |
| <ItemImage id="energizedpower:solar_panel_7"/> Tier VII | 16,000 FE/t (524,288) |

> <Color id="yellow">Connect Tier III and IV straight to storage.</Color> Tier III transfers only 64 FE/t (512), and Tier IV holds only 256 FE (81,920).
