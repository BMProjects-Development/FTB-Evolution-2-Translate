  # <Color id="gold">Power Generation Changes</Color>

  <ItemImage id="powah:energy_cell_nitro" scale="2"/>

  FTB Evolution 2 rebalances power across every tech mod so that no single generator makes the others pointless. This guide lists what the pack changed. Anything not listed here works the way its own mod describes it.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">The Big Rules</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

* **Lava is worth 10,000 FE per bucket** in the Oritech Lava Generator, the Energized Power Thermal Generator and the Powah Magmator.
* **Water is not a reactor coolant.** Powah Thermo Generators and Reactors run on Industrial Coolant, made by dropping Dry Ice into a water source.
* **There is one steam.** Colossal Reactors make Oritech steam, and their turbines and the Oritech Steam Engine both burn it.
* **Industrial Contraptions runs on FE.** Its generators and machines connect to any other mod's cables, with no Energy Rectifier needed.
* **Some early generators are gone**, so each mod's power line starts where the pack wants it to.
