<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">XP Sources</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

| Action | Athletics XP |
|---|---|
| Sprinting on the ground | about 20 per second |
| Climbing up a ladder, vine or other climbable block | about 20 per second |
| Swimming (sprinting in water) | about 2 per second |
| Jumping | 1 per jump |
| Catching a fish | 5 per catch |
| Catching a Starcatcher fish | 10 per fish |
| Winning a Nautec lucky zone minigame | 10 per win |

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Movement pays for every tick you are actually moving, so standing still or riding something gives nothing. Walking, sneaking, paddling on the surface and walking underwater do not count. Only sprinting, climbing upward and sprint swimming do.
