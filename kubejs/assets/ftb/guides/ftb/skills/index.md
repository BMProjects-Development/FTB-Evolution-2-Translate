<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  # <Color id="gold">Skill Trees</Color>

  <ItemImage id="minecraft:experience_bottle" scale="2"/>

  FTB Evolution 2 has five skill trees: Melee, Ranged, Athletics, Mining and Magic. Each one levels from its own kind of play, and every level gives you a skill point to spend in that tree.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Open the trees with <KeyBind id="key.puffish_skills.open"/>. Every tree starts with one free point.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

> <Color id="yellow">Note</Color>: this is skill XP, not vanilla experience. Experience orbs do nothing for a tree, and spending levels at an anvil or enchanting table takes nothing from one.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
