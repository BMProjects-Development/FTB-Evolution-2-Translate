<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  # <Color id="light_purple">Magic</Color>

  <ItemImage id="arsmagicalegacy:spell_book" scale="2"/>

  Magic levels from every magic mod in the pack, not just Ars Magica. Rituals, rites, brews, soul work and enchanting all count, so pick whichever school you enjoy.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

> <Color id="yellow">Note</Color>: Ars Magica: Legacy has its own magic level and skill points for its spell parts. That is a separate system. Each time it goes up a level, the Magic tree gets XP as well.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Any School</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

| Action | Magic XP |
|---|---|
| Magic damage to a mob | 0.5 per point of damage |
| Enchanting an item at an <ItemLink id="minecraft:enchanting_table"/> | 30 |

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Magic damage covers vanilla magic (Harming, Poison clouds and the like), Ars Magica: Legacy spells, Roots Classic spells and EvilCraft's Vengeance Beam. Hits on a target dummy give nothing.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
