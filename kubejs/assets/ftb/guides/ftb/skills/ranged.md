  ## <Color id="gold">XP Sources</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

| Damage from | Ranged XP |
|---|---|
| Iron's Arms 'n Artifice bullets | 0.4 per point of damage |
| LTX Industries guns, grenades and rockets | 0.4 per point of damage |
| The <ItemLink id="nautec:atlantean_rifle"/> beam | 0.4 per point of damage |
| Any other projectile (arrows, tridents, thrown items) | 0.25 per point of damage |

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Killing a mob with any of these pays the same kill XP as Melee: up to 10 from its experience drop, twice the square root of anything past 10, and a fifth of its maximum health up to 40. See [Melee](melee.md) for examples.

Hits on a target dummy give nothing.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

> <Color id="yellow">Note</Color>: only the first 375 damage you deal to any one mob counts toward XP, and that resets after 5 minutes. Kills follow the same limit as Melee, 15 per chunk every 5 minutes, so spread out to keep earning.
