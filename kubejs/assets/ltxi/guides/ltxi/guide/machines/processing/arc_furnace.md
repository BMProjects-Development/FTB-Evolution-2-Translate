# Arc Furnace

<BlockImage id="arc_furnace" scale="4" />

## About

This high temperature digital smelter can alloy metals and other elemental composites like phosphorus-based neon lights.
It can also smelt metal dusts like Tungsten-Plated Slatesteel or Rhenium which require shielding gas and much more heat
than a regular furnace can provide.

## Recipe Spec

- Base crafting time: varies, by default 10 seconds
- Inputs: 1-3 items, 0-1 fluid(s)
- Outputs: 1 item

## Compatible Upgrades

Installed via the [Upgrade System](../../fundamentals/upgrade_system.md).

- **Σ/VM3 Power Supply Unit**: Max rank 5. Each rank halves the time per operation and raises
  energy capacity and transfer rate. Energy usage doubles with every rank.
- **Σ/4D Hypervisor Module**: Max rank 3. Processes 4 operations per cycle at rank 1, 8 at
  rank 2, and 12 at rank 3.
