## About
The Digital Cooking Machine trio consists of the **Digital Furnace**, **Digital Smoker**, and **Digital Blast Furnace**.
As you might guess from their names, they are [upgradeable](../../fundamentals/upgrade_system.md) LTX machine variants
of the base game's cooking blocks.

## Recipe Spec
- Base crafting time: Varies. Uses Vanilla recipe cook times.
- Inputs: 1 item
- Outputs: 1 item

## Compatible Upgrades

- **Σ/VM3 Power Supply Unit**: Max rank 5. Each rank halves the time per operation and raises
  energy capacity and transfer rate. Energy usage doubles with every rank.
- **Σ/4D Hypervisor Module**: Max rank 3. Processes 4 operations per cycle at rank 1, 8 at
  rank 2, and 12 at rank 3.
