# ElectroCentrifuge

<BlockImage id="electrocentrifuge" scale="4" />

## About

One of our most prized inventions, an Electrolysis and Centrifuging all-in-one machine. This machine specializes in
extracting base elements from all sorts of materials.

## Recipe Spec

- Base crafting time: varies, by default 10 seconds
- Inputs: 0-1 items, 0-1 fluids
- Outputs: 0-4 items, 0-2 fluids

## Compatible Upgrades

Installed via the [Upgrade System](../../fundamentals/upgrade_system.md).

- **Σ/VM3 Power Supply Unit**: Max rank 5. Each rank halves the time per operation and raises
  energy capacity and transfer rate. Energy usage doubles with every rank.
- **Σ/4D Hypervisor Module**: Max rank 3. Processes 4 operations per cycle at rank 1, 8 at
  rank 2, and 12 at rank 3.
- **Ore Crystallizing Tubes**: Max rank 1. Unlocks the **Ore Processing** [recipe mode](../../fundamentals/recipe_modes.md),
  the fifth step of the ore processing chain.
