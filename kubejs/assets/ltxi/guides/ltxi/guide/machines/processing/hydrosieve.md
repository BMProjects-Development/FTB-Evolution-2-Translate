# HydroSieve

<BlockImage id="hydrosieve" scale="4" />

## About

Our research department found that flowing water improves yield, efficiency, and automation of sieves compared to traditional
dry setups.

## Recipe Spec

- Base crafting time: varies, by default 10s
- Inputs: 1 item, 1 fluid
- Outputs: 1-6 items

## Compatible Upgrades

Installed via the [Upgrade System](../../fundamentals/upgrade_system.md).

- **Σ/VM3 Power Supply Unit**: Max rank 5. Each rank halves the time per operation and raises
  energy capacity and transfer rate. Energy usage doubles with every rank.
- **Σ/4D Hypervisor Module**: Max rank 3. Processes 4 operations per cycle at rank 1, 8 at
  rank 2, and 12 at rank 3.
- **Ore Washing Kit**: Max rank 1. Unlocks the **Ore Processing** [recipe mode](../../fundamentals/recipe_modes.md),
  the second step of the ore processing chain.
