# Voltaic Injector

<BlockImage id="voltaic_injector" scale="4" />

## About

Blasts material with a beam of extremely high voltage. It can force mutations of certain dyes into specialty pigments,
cut clean ore materials, and blue titanium glass.

## Recipe Spec
- Base crafting time: varies, by default 10 seconds
- Inputs: 1 item
- Outputs: 1-3 items

## Compatible Upgrades

Installed via the [Upgrade System](../../fundamentals/upgrade_system.md).

- **Σ/VM3 Power Supply Unit**: Max rank 5. Each rank halves the time per operation and raises
  energy capacity and transfer rate. Energy usage doubles with every rank.
- **Σ/4D Hypervisor Module**: Max rank 3. Processes 4 operations per cycle at rank 1, 8 at
  rank 2, and 12 at rank 3.
- **Ore Cutting Laser**: Max rank 1. Unlocks the **Ore Processing** [recipe mode](../../fundamentals/recipe_modes.md),
  the third step of the ore processing chain.
