# <Color id="green">Nuclear Reactor</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  # <Color id="green">Nuclear Reactor</Color>

  <ItemImage id="oritech:nuclear_reactor_controller" scale="2"/>

  The <Color id="green">Nuclear Reactor</Color> consumes <Color id="yellow">uranium</Color> or <Color id="light_purple">plutonium</Color> pellets to generate large amounts of <Color id="gold">RF</Color>. The Output scales with the number of fuel rods and neutron reflectors inside. Reactors must be kept sufficiently cooled or they will melt down.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Structure</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

The reactor is built as a **rectangular hollow box** of <Color id="green">Reactor Walls</Color>. A few rules to keep in mind:

- Maximum size is **64 blocks per direction**
- The <Color id="green">Reactor Controller</Color> and <Color id="green">Energy Ports</Color> must be placed somewhere on the **outer walls**, an optional <Color id="green">Redstone Ports</Color> may also go on these **outer walls**.
- <Color id="green">Fuel Ports</Color> and <Color id="green">Coolant Ports</Color> go on the **outer ceiling**, above the relevant interior stacks
- All other components (rods, pipes, vents, absorbers) go **inside**
- The interior must be **identical on every vertical layer**; Design your layout and scale the height as desired. Power output, fuel consumption, and coolant usage all scale with height

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Components</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="oritech:nuclear_reactor_controller"/>
  ### <Color id="aqua">Reactor Controller</Color>
</Row>

The <Color id="green">Reactor Controller</Color> is the heart of the reactor. Place it anywhere on the outer walls, then interact with it to assemble or update the structure. The GUI displays current status, component temperatures, and output statistics.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="oritech:reactor_wall"/>
  ### <Color id="aqua">Reactor Wall</Color>
</Row>

<Color id="green">Reactor Walls</Color> form the blast-resistant outer shell of the reactor. They make up the entire frame: top, bottom, and sides.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="oritech:reactor_rod"/>
  ### <Color id="aqua">Reactor Rods</Color>
</Row>

<ItemGrid>
  <ItemIcon id="oritech:reactor_rod"/>
  <ItemIcon id="oritech:reactor_double_rod"/>
  <ItemIcon id="oritech:reactor_quad_rod"/>
</ItemGrid>

<Color id="gold">Reactor Rods</Color> are the fuel-burning interior components. They come in <Color id="green">single</Color>, <Color id="yellow">double</Color>, and <Color id="red">quad</Color> variants, each generating more heat and RF per tick. Every rod stack needs a <Color id="green">Fuel Port</Color> placed directly above it on the outer ceiling.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="oritech:reactor_neutron_reflector"/>
  ### <Color id="aqua">Neutron Reflector</Color>
</Row>

<Color id="green">Neutron Reflectors</Color> are best placed next to fuel rods to reflect neutrons back into the rod, boosting their efficiency without needing to add another rod. A great way to increase output in a compact layout.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="oritech:reactor_fuel_port"/>
  ### <Color id="aqua">Reactor Fuel Port</Color>
</Row>

The <Color id="green">Reactor Fuel Port</Color> is placed on the **outer ceiling** directly above a rod stack. Insert <Color id="yellow">Uranium Pellets</Color> or <Color id="light_purple">Plutonium Pellets</Color> here to keep the rods burning.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="oritech:reactor_energy_port"/>
  ### <Color id="aqua">Reactor Energy Port</Color>
</Row>

<Color id="green">Reactor Energy Ports</Color> are placed on the outer walls to output the RF generated by the reactor. Each port can output up to <Color id="gold">50,000,000 RF/t</Color>.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="oritech:reactor_redstone_port"/>
  ### <Color id="aqua">Reactor Redstone Port</Color>
</Row>

The <Color id="green">Reactor Redstone Port</Color> is placed on the outer walls. Applying a redstone signal will prevent new fuel from being loaded. Any pellet already burning will finish first. Right-clicking the port cycles its comparator output mode between <Color id="gold">Energy</Color>, <Color id="aqua">Active Rod Count</Color>, and <Color id="red">Temperature</Color>.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
