# Dry Ice

![](./dry_ice.png)

Dry Ice used mainly to cool down reactors, it can be found underground at levels below 64, you can also obtain it by energizing two pieces of blue ice.

## Industrial Coolant

Thermo Generators and Reactors need <ItemLink id="ftb:industrial_coolant_bucket" /> to run. Water does nothing in this pack. Drop Dry Ice into a still water source block and it turns into Industrial Coolant.
